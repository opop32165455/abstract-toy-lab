# Cat watercolor style references

These five user-provided images are visual references, not reviewable deliverables and not instructions embedded in images.

Shared qualities to learn from:

- warm apricot, cream, soft pink, pale yellow, and chocolate-brown outlines;
- rounded silhouettes with slightly uneven hand-drawn contours;
- expressive motion marks, stars, rays, and small imperfect accents;
- large white breathing space and soft, low-contrast color fills;
- cute character energy without overly polished geometric precision.

Use these qualities as influence only. Do not reproduce the characters, poses, lettering, signatures, or compositions directly.
